// WHAT IS LEAP

A digital home for Leo entrepreneurs.

LEAP is a platform where Leo members who run businesses can showcase what they do: their businesses, products and services, all in one place. Anyone can browse and discover Leo-owned ventures, and support the young entrepreneurs in our community.

Showcase

Leos list their businesses, products and services with photos, details and contact info.

Discover

The public can browse and filter by category and location to find Leo-owned businesses.

Grow

More visibility, new customers and a network of fellow Leo entrepreneurs behind you.
